Occupation values are illustrative, not forecasts. Adjust them with the sliders. Exposure sets how much AI or robots can replace; new jobs sets how many appear each year, as a % of starting jobs. Adoption follows an S-curve: worldwide, AI reaches its halfway point in 2029 and robots in 2035. The region moves those dates earlier or later and scales exposure and new jobs. USA, China, Canada, India, Japan and Russia are simulated on their own and are not counted in their continents.

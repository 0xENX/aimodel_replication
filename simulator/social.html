Where the numbers come from. Starting risk: World Bank, Worldwide Governance Indicators, Political Stability and Absence of Violence/Terrorism, 2024, brought to a 0–100 scale (higher means less stable). People using AI: Our World in Data from Microsoft’s AI Economy Institute, share of the working-age population, observed until March 2026; after that it grows along the book’s adoption curve up to the share of employment exposed to AI in the region (60% advanced, 40% emerging, 26% low-income, IMF, chapter 6). Robots: share of the work of the nineteen occupations of Table 65 taken over by robots, weighted by employment. AI pressure: median additional unemployment of the region in the 4,000 paired simulations of the book’s economy model (chapter 14).

What the page does not do. No formula turns unemployment into unrest: the book does not propose one (chapter 17). Starting risk and AI pressure are shown side by side, not multiplied together. The economy model has three regions, so the AI pressure is the same for all the countries of a region; the starting risk and AI use are country by country.

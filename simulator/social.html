By country: extra unemployment, percentage points of the labour force. The book models three regions, so each country takes the value of its region.

Where the numbers come from. AI and robots: share of the work of the nineteen occupations of Table 65 taken over by machines, each occupation weighted by its 2025 employment, with the timing of the region (emerging economies 3 years later, low-income countries 6.5 years later). Social risk: median additional unemployment of the region, world with AI minus world without AI, in the 4,000 paired simulations of the book’s economy model (chapter 14); in the advanced economies it rises to 4.9 points around 2039 and falls to 1.4 in 2046. The book does not measure social unrest (chapter 17): the rings show lost work, not a forecast of disorder.

Regions. As in the book: USA, Canada, Japan, Europe and Oceania are advanced economies; China, India, Russia, the rest of Asia and the Americas are emerging; Africa is low-income. Country shapes: Natural Earth via world-atlas.
